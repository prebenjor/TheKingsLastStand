# Shops and equipment

Canonical details are in docs/ITEMS-AND-EQUIPMENT.md and docs/ITEM-CATALOG.md. The current generator has 80 tiered equipment entries across 16 families and five qualities, three crafted items, nine attribute tomes, and four boss relic object IDs. The native target is the Forsaken Kingdom backpack with 30 storage and nine equipment slots.

Known player report: shops have appeared empty or text-only, and purchased items have not equipped through the backpack. Do not mark this resolved until current-build shop icon/tooltips, purchase ownership, native storage, equip/unequip stats, and death persistence pass in Warcraft III.
