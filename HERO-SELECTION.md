# Hero selection

At match start, players choose one of 15 hero previews before the first preparation countdown. Duplicate selections are permitted. Players have 45 seconds; anyone who does not confirm receives Paladin. After all active players confirm, initial preparation lasts 45 seconds. Every hero starts at level 3.

The hero race does not change the player's Human workers or building tree. Read docs/HEROES-AND-ABILITIES.md for all hero identities, native skill IDs, signature spell effects, and progression.

The selector is present in development source, but the exact current build still needs its editor round-trip and Custom Game startup checks.
