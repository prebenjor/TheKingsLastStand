# King Aldric's Castle

King Aldric is the shared survival objective. Select/click his castle to use its native command-card controls; current design requires clear Heal King and Royal Defense Upgrade buttons with short tooltips.

- Heal costs 150 personal gold + 50 personal lumber and restores up to 2,000 health. Full-health attempts refund.
- Five upgrades cost 400 + 200 per existing tier gold and 150 lumber each; every tier adds 4,000 maximum/current health and 30 damage.
- Every transaction is atomic and uses the acting defender's resources.

Live native command-card layout, cost display, refund, and king stat effects are pending for build KLS-D-fd638ddcf5. Full requirement: docs/GAMEPLAY-SPEC.md.
